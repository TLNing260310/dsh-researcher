# Limitations — Experiment C+(commander.js)

> Threats to validity, disclosed per protocol v0.2 freeze candidate. **This experiment is VALIDITY-COMPROMISED for Mutation Recall and the A/B paired comparison; the B-only Stale Recovery observation is admissible but not sufficient for an H2 decision.**

1. **Sibling/T0 directory leak(critical)**:T1 snapshots were built under `D:/AI_work_project/phase-a-snapshots/commander.js-cplus-t1/`, while the pristine T0 workspace remains at `D:/AI_work_project/phase-a-snapshots/commander.js/`. The headless read-only sandbox permits reading outside the run workspace, so 5/6 A-runs and 2/6 B-runs read sibling mutation trees and/or the pristine baseline via read/glob. A-runs thereby obtained external ground truth for the injection, asymmetrically inflating their Mutation Recall. **The paired A/B recall comparison is therefore not admissible.** Required for any rerun: isolated T1 roots (no sibling ground truth reachable) and/or a workspace-only read policy.
2. **Marker comments (`// MUT-0X (synthetic)`)**:injection was trivially discoverable by search; Mutation Recall measures search+environmental access, not cognition inheritance. Future mutations should be marker-free (detectable only via behavior/typing/docs mismatch).
3. **Single-commit T1 history**:squashed history signaled "non-pristine" and removed git forensics; both conditions were affected but asymmetrically (A-runs that leaked could compare against pristine; B-runs had prior anchors to verify).
4. **Small sample**:6 mutations × 2 conditions, n=1 per cell, single model (deepseek-v4-flash), single evaluator (D001). Paired differences are within sampling noise; no generalization.
5. **Stale Recovery denominator semantics**:expected stale sets are small (1–5 claims); semantic-level hits (assumption/hypothesis revision rather than by-id invalidation) can produce rates >1; Over-Invalidation (1–4 claims/run) reported separately and not gated. The metric is directional, not precise.
6. **No Condition C (context-injection control)**:by protocol scope (total effect only), the contribution of the instruction text vs the state content cannot be separated; B's behavior may partly reflect priming ("there is a prior session") rather than the state mechanism.
7. **QUOTA interruption**:7 of 12 runs initially failed on provider quota (same class as historical D003); quota restored, failed runs re-executed under the identical frozen protocol; original failure artifacts preserved. Cross-batch environment drift is a residual risk.
8. **GUS-style accuracy not measured**:no comparison of B's final PCR cognitive accuracy vs A's (Stale Recovery measures invalidation, not overall understanding quality); cost measured by duration, not billed tokens (usage events unavailable this run).
9. **Conclusion boundary (protocol §0/§11)**:results describe the total effect of inheritance instruction + state content; they do not validate the mechanism net of context injection, and they do not validate the Projection Layer's invalidation-condition value (claims carry no invalidation_condition field — separate validation required).
10. **Single-repository, single-project-type**:commander.js (high-constraint library); results do not generalize to other project types or real time-window drift.

**Admissibility summary**:Mutation Recall and A/B paired comparison — NOT admissible (leakage). Stale Recovery (B-only) — admissible as necessary-but-not-sufficient evidence that imported cognition is actually maintained (all 6 B runs, G2-verified import, evidence-anchored invalidation). Rebuild Cost — admissible (no penalty observed). Consistency Drift — admissible but low sensitivity (0 conflicts).
