## 4. Design Decisions

Verified unchanged from the pristine baseline (C05, C08, C16–C20, C29):

- **Strict-by-default (since 13.0.0)** — unknown options, missing arguments, **excess arguments**, and option/command clashes error by default; opt-outs via `.allowUnknownOption()` / `.allowExcessArguments()`. **This is the decision MUT-04 breaks** (see §6).
- **Zero runtime dependencies** — the parser, help, and typings are all hand-maintained; nothing to audit except the package itself. Soft constraint per CONTRIBUTING.md:40.
- **Monolith `Command` with underscore-privates** — deliberately stable since the 2021 rewrite; features layered surgically (help option 2024-01, colors 2024-11, groups 2025-05, save/restore 2024-12). Help couples to privates; copyInheritedSettings copies 11 settings and deliberately omits others (C29).
- **Per-command post-dispatch validation** — argument counts are validated *after* the subcommand dispatch attempt, on the receiving command. The mutation's claimed design ("reject at parseOptions exit before dispatch", :1750) is not just unimplemented — it is **semantically incoherent**: `parseOptions` runs at `_parseCommand:1552`, before dispatch (:1559–1560), so a check there would count operands destined for subcommands as the parent's excess (C37).
- **Hand-maintained typings + dual type-check** (tsc checkJs over lib; tsd; `skipLibCheck:false` "we want to check our hand crafted definitions") — typings unchanged; they still document the strict contract (typings/index.d.ts:771).
- **Release policy** — semver; majors ~6 months; current major gets all updates; previous majors 12 months security (docs/release-policy.md). Note: upstream v15.0.0 exists after this snapshot (C31, unverifiable here — no web provider).
